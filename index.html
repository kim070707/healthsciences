About CAGE

Standing with people. Challenging injustice.

CAGE works to empower communities through advocacy, research, public education and support for people affected by discriminatory policies and practices.

01 / ADVOCACY

Challenge injustice

Highlighting policies and practices that undermine rights, dignity and the rule of law.

02 / RESEARCH

Inform the debate

Producing research and publications to document issues and bring overlooked experiences into public discussion.

03 / EMPOWERMENT

Support communities

Sharing knowledge, resources and platforms that help individuals and communities make their voices heard.

Our principles

A just society begins by listening to those most affected, defending human dignity and holding power to account.
